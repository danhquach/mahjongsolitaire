# Try our game — about 10 minutes

Thanks for helping. This is an early test build, so rough edges are expected — finding them is
exactly what you're here for. **Nothing you do can break it**, and there's no wrong way to play.

**Link:** https://lantern-tiles.dqtgametesting.workers.dev

Nothing to install, no account, no sign-up. It opens in your web browser.

---

## Before you start

1. **Use a laptop, desktop computer, or a tablet.** Please skip your phone this round — we already
   know the board is too cramped on a phone screen and we're fixing it separately.
2. **If you're on a tablet, turn it sideways** (landscape). The board is built for a wide screen.
3. **Open the link in a private window** so you start fresh:
   - Chrome / Edge: `Ctrl+Shift+N` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+N` (Mac)
   - Safari: File → New Private Window
   - Firefox: `Ctrl+Shift+P` / `Cmd+Shift+P`

   If that's fiddly, don't worry about it — just tell us you skipped this step.

## What we'd like you to do

**Play one full level — clear every tile off the board.**

Please **don't look up how to play, and don't ask anyone for help.** We're testing whether the game
explains itself. If you get confused, that's useful information, not a failure on your part.

**Note roughly how long it takes you.** The clock starts on your first tap on the board and stops
when you see "Level complete!".

**If you're still going after about 5 minutes, just stop.** Note how many tiles were left (the
count is at the top of the screen). A stop is a perfectly good result for us.

---

## Then tell us

Short answers are fine — a couple of sentences each.

1. Did you finish? How long did it take? If you stopped, how many tiles were left?
2. What was confusing, at any point?
3. Was anything hard to **see** — too small, too faint, hard to tell apart?
4. Did anything feel broken, slow, or unresponsive?
5. Would you play this again? Why or why not?

**Please also tell us:** what device and browser you used (e.g. "iPad, Safari" or "Windows laptop,
Chrome").
